Earth, the beautiful planet we call home, is the only known place in the universe where life thrives. Its plentiful resources and distinct biomes have led to an abundance of life, Flora and Fauna alike. OBJECTIVE The objective in Abundance remains the same: players compete to earn as many Victory Points (VP) as possible. To do so, you will plant cards to create your own tableau: a 16-card grid arranged 4 cards wide by 4 cards tall. The game ends at the end of a round after a player plants their 16th card, which completes their tableau. Players will score points for the value of Flora and Event cards, for Growth and Sprouts placed on their Flora, for cards in their Compost, and for Ecosystem, Fauna, and Terrain bonus points. The first player to fill their 4x4 tableau with 16 cards triggers the game end and earns a bonus of 11VP. Abundance is an expansion; you need the base game Earth to play. Abundance works at all player counts and adds the option to play with a 6th player; however, if you do, you must use the expansion rules and components or you may run out of cards and/or components. NEW ICONS AND EFFECTS Not only does Abundance offer more ways for players to plan ahead and convert resources, it also adds a new layer of player interaction. New Events and Abilities can affect the card s owner and their opponents. Some cards simultaneously grant resources for you and your opponents, while others capitalize on the cards in your neighbour s tableau. There is also a new type of Event which can affect all players but has a timing restraint: Play at the end of a turn. Each player who wishes to pay the cost in full will receive the benefit in full. The Event card is placed in the Event space of the player whose hand it came from. You can gain additional Seed tokens by activating abilities or at any time by converting your Leaf tokens or Sprouts: 1 Leaf for 1 Seed, or 4 Sprouts for 1 Seed. Sprouts are returned to the central supply, while Leaves are removed from play. Players should be aware that Leaves cannot reenter play therefore, by converting a Leaf token into a Seed, a player will have fewer Leaf tokens available to claim Fauna objectives and/or the tableau completion bonus. Similar to converting Sprouts to Soil, at any time players can convert 1 Seed into any one of the following: a new Germinate action (see below), 2 Soil, 2 Growth, 3 Sprouts, 3 Compost from draw pile, or 4 Compost from hand. If Seed tokens should run out, use a temporary replacement. SOLO MODE The solo mode objective is the same as the base game. During setup, add 10 Sprouts to the Gaia board, take 2 Seeds, place the 11VP token on the Fauna board, and place the solo Abundance board faceup under your player board. Changes to game play: When another/other player should receive Sprout(s), place them directly on Gaia s board. Play at the end of a turn Events will only affect you, not Gaia. Costs must be paid in full when the card is played. For cards that give you benefits depending on your neighbour s tableau, reference your own tableau. Each time you convert a Leaf to a Seed, place the Leaf on Gaia s board. She ll score 5VP per Leaf on her board at the end of the game. When you calculate scores, you can record Gaia s VP from Leaves on one of her unused Ecosystem scoring spaces. Germinate is a powerful new action that allows you to find a card with a specific scoring objective. Simply return one Seed token to the supply and announce one standard scoring objective. (For a list of standard objectives see the back page or player aids.) Next, take the draw pile and turn it faceup so the bottom card is faceup on top of the deck. Make sure other players can see this card. Starting with the topmost card, check each card one at a time for the objective that you ve chosen. You must take the first card that matches that objective and place it in your hand. All other cards that were checked must be put into the discard pile. If you are unable to find a card matching your objective after searching the entire draw pile, you may then search the discard pile. If a card with the matching scoring objective still cannot be found, this action is considered incomplete and you retrieve your Seed token from the supply - it is immediately available for use. If the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile to create a new draw pile. Once a scoring objective has been announced, it cannot be changed. These Sprouts cannot be used until they have been moved onto empty Sprout spaces on Flora. SPROUTS CAN BE SAVED! Teammates still sit beside each other. In a 2vs2 game, use the Teams board from the base game. In 2vs2vs2 or 3vs3 mode, use the appropriate side of the expansion Teams board. Any time a player earns resources (including Seeds), instead of placing them directly into their tableau, reserve, or hand, they may place them onto their side of the shared Team board. These resources must be taken by either teammate before the end of the current turn or they are discarded. Play the rest of the game as normal with the exception of . When selecting one other player, it must be an opponent. You cannot choose your teammate. At the end of the game, teammates will simply combine their scores, and the team with the most VP wins!
