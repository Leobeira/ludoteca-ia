Game Components and overview As spring dawns, the great thaw begins. Deep beneath the lush meadow grass, the queen stirs in her nest and the colony comes to life. Soldiers venture forth, battling centipedes and clashing with opposing colonies for territory. Workers dig an ever-expanding network of tunnels in their tireless search for food. The first larvae hatch, and it is clear this generation will be different: the young ants rapidly evolve into a multitude of new forms. The ants march out to claim the meadow as their own. GAME OVERVIEW March of the Ants is a civilization game where each player grows an ant colony. You will explore the meadow, gather resources, evolve a unique ant species, and march to battle. There are many different paths to victory. Throughout the game you will gain Colony Points by expanding your territory, evolving your ants, meeting colony goals, and battling. At the end of the game, the player with the most points will rule the Meadow! March of the Ants is played over several rounds, each of which has 4 distinct phases: W orker Phase, where you take turns taking actions. Soldier Phase, where battles are fought in contested locations. Queen Phase, where you harvest resources and feed your ants. Slumber, where you score points for your achievements during the round. Ant, Larva, or Egg? The wooden ant tokens represent different stages of the life cycle of ants, depending on where they are located. When tokens are in the Meadow (the Great Tunnel or hexes), they are ants. When they are in your Larvae Chamber, they are larvae. All other tokens are kept to the side of the Nest Mat as eggs. GAIN LARVA Take an egg from your supply and place it in your Larvae Chamber. MOVE LARVA Take a larva from your Larvae Chamber and place it into the Meadow, where it immediately becomes an ant. LIMITED ANTS Each player has a limit of 36 wooden ant tokens available to them each game. If they are all in use, the player cannot gain more larva or ants. Game Variants 1 45 the worker phase The Great Tunnel The Great Tunnel is the center of the Meadow and the origin of all of the ant colonies. A battle will take place here during the Soldier Phase if multiple players ants are present. GREAT TUNNEL RULES The Great Tunnel does not count as a hex. Hexes adjacent to the Great Tunnel grant to their controller during Slumber. When moving larvae, players may always move them to the Great Tunnel. Overview During the Worker Phase, players take turns in clockwise order, starting with the player with the Active Player Marker. Play continues until all faceup Rest Tiles have been taken using the Rest action. At the end of the Worker Phase, each player with an evolved thorax will get to take one or more bonus reactions. Taking a Turn On your turn, choose an action to perform. ACTION Explore, Forage, March, Play a Card, or Rest. You first pay the cost, then perform the action. REACTIONS After you take an action, the 2 neighboring players may take the associated reaction. Reactions have no cost and are taken in clockwise order from the Active Player. The player to your left goes first, then the player to the right. After your action and the reactions are resolved, pass the Active Player Marker clockwise, and the next turn begins. The Meadow is the game board where most of the action takes place in March of the Ants. It is made up of distinct locations, which include the Great Tunnel and all placed hexes. It will expand during the game as you Explore and place new hexes. Hexes Hexes have different features, collection sites, and sometimes special rules. Ants in a hex will first inhabit collection sites but if all sites are full may reside in the outskirts. THE GREAT TUNNELTHE GREAT TUNNEL COLLECTION SITE Offers valuable resources that ants may collect during the Queen Phase. Each site may be inhabited by at most one ant. CONTROL SITE A type of collection site that also grants control of the hex. Control of a hex is important for scoring, and during battle. OUTSKIRTS Anywhere in the hex that is not a site. Ants may occupy the outskirts of a hex only if there are no empty collection sites in that hex. They do not inhabit the hex or harvest resources, but do participate in battle. NAME AND NUMBER Hex numbers are used in the Soldier Phase, and are color-coded according to their number. Hex names do not have any gameplay effect. TUNNELS Ants may move between adjacent locations if those locations have a connected tunnel. BEYOND THE MEADOW EFFECTS Beyond the Meadow hexes have unique effects. Each has its special effect written on it. The player who places the hex resolves any when placed effects before any players take reactions. Beyond the Meadow hexes are part of the Meadow, despite their name. ConCepts: hex rules Placing a Hex Anytime you place a hex in the Meadow, whether through Exploration, a card effect, or another ability: You must connect at least one of its tunnel openings to an existing tunnel opening. You may block its tunnels or the tunnels of other hexes. If the hex has a feature (wormhole or centipede) place the corresponding token on the hex. Resolve any special abilities. Inhabiting a Hex When you have at least one ant in a collection site in a hex, you inhabit that hex. Inhabiting a hex allows you to move larvae directly to that hex when moving larvae, and is required for many card effects and abilities. Movement and Hexes When you move an ant into a hex, it immediately inhabits an empty collection site of your choice. If none are empty, place the ant in the outskirts (anywhere else on that hex). When an ant leaves a collection site, any ant in the outskirts of that hex will immediately inhabit it at the end of the current action or reaction. If multiple players have ants in the outskirts, players take turns inhabiting as many of the empty sites as they can in player order. Sharing & Contesting Hexes Ants from multiple colonies may peacefully share a hex as long as there are enough collection sites for all the ants. A hex is shared when 2 or more players ants inhabit that hex. If a hex is shared, but there are not enough sites for all the ants, that hex is also contested. During the Soldier Phase, there will be a battle in each contested hex. Worker Phase Actions There are 5 Worker Phase actions that you may choose from on your turn. CENTIPEDES A hex can only have 1 centipede token on it at a time. Centipedes may not be placed in the Great Tunnel. CONTESTED HEX If multiple players have ants in the same hex and there are ants in the outskirts, it is contested. If a centipede and ants are in the same hex, it is contested. If a hex is contested during Soldier Phase, a battle takes place there. CONTROL SITE A site in a hex that gives the occupying ant s player control over that hex, allowing them to win ties in battle and gain 1 point in Slumber if the hex is adjacent to the Great Tunnel. EXCESS CASUALTIES Once all casualties have been assigned, any extra have no effect. GREAT DEFENDER REST TILE This Rest Tile is available each round. The player with this tile counts as the defender in the Great Tunnel Battle, even if they are not present in the battle. Ties are broken in clockwise order, starting with them, and Ferocity card play starts with the player to their left. THE GREAT TUNNEL The central location of the Meadow. You may always move larvae into the Great Tunnel. A battle will take place there during Soldier Phase if ants from 2 or more different colonies are present. The Great Tunnel is not a hex. HAND A player s cards. There is no hand size limit. The number of cards each player has is public knowledge. HEX BATTLE A battle that takes place in a hex. (As opposed to the Great Tunnel Battle, which is not a hex battle.) INHABIT A hex is inhabited by each player who has at least one ant in a collection site in that hex. THE MEADOW Consists of the Great Tunnel and all placed hexes. MOVING LARVAE When a larva moves to the Meadow, it immediately becomes an ant. When taking steps, you may move larva directly to any hex you inhabit or the Great Tunnel. LOCATION Each hex and the Great Tunnel. LOSE ANTS Remove your own ants from the Meadow. When you are instructed to lose ants, as a result of a card effect or battle casualties, you choose which ants to lose. OUTER MEADOW HEX A hex that is not adjacent to the Great Tunnel. OUTSKIRTS Any place in a hex that is not a collection site. If all collection sites in a hex are inhabited, any ant that enters that hex is placed in the outskirts. Ants in the outskirts of a hex automatically move to inhabit collection sites that become empty as soon as the current action or reaction is resolved. PLAYER ORDER The order in which turns are taken. Player order is clockwise and starts with the Active Player unless specified otherwise. PLAY PRIORITY Unless specified, resolve questions of priority in player order. REACTIONS Players only take reactions to their neighbors' main action that they take on their turn. Players do not take reactions to action-like effects that result from cards or other abilities. SHARED HEX A hex that 2 or more players inhabit. WORMHOLE HEX A hex that contains a wormhole token is sometimes referred to as a wormhole hex. WORMHOLES A location with a wormhole token has a wormhole. Ants may move from any hex with a wormhole to any other hex with a wormhole with 1 step. Ants may not Explore through wormholes. A Note On Scientific Names The scientific names shown on the Evolution cards include a real life genus and a fictional species. The attributes and characteristics of each ant genus were used as creative inspiration for the Evolution abilities. 2223 Worker Phase Actions EXPLORE Add a new hex to the Meadow.
