EXAMPLE The Orange player performs the Local Markets action. They decide to place 2 Influence tokens in the Region with the bonus. For their first activation, the Orange player spends 1 from their Paper Factory to place their Influence token as normal. They want to interact with the Genro later this turn, so instead of gaining the Region bonus (2 ) or 2 , they gain the Genro s approval placing their Loyalty token, dark side up, on one of the Loyalty boards. Since the Orange player spent 1 from the Paper Factory and there are no Loyalty tokens in the Paper column yet, they must place the token on the lowermost empty space in that column. For their second activation, the Orange player spends 3 from their Lens Factory and decides again to gain Genro s approval in order to interact with the other Genro later this turn. Since they spent 3 from the Lens Factory, they would normally place a Loyalty token on the topmost space of the Lens column. However, there s an empty space below that one, so the Orange player places their Loyalty token there instead. REVISED BASE GAME RULES These rules apply both with and without the Genro expansion. Build a Factory You may build up to two Factories of the same type, but you may not have more than two Factories of the same level. (The Old Factory is considered a 1-level Factory.) Build a Ship You may build a Ship even if your Workers area has no empty space for an Expert Worker. In that case, do not take the Expert Worker from the chosen Destination (but still take the Upgraded Department token from there as normal). If your Workers area has an empty space, you must place the Expert Worker there as normal.
