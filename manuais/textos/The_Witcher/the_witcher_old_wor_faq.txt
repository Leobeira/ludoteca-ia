3 1.   “As a result of your Attack” When an effect inflicts Damage out of a Com - bo, does it trigger effects that call for an At - tack? Or are Attacks considered to be the  Combo and nothing else? Answer:  Any effect that says “as a result of  your Attack” is triggered when you damage  your opponent, no matter the source/effect.  Sometimes your Shield level is high enough  to absorb all the Damage, and you do not have  to discard a card. That’s why we decided to use  the sentence “to discard a card as a result of an  opponent’s attack”. 2.   Return this card to your hand  The effect “Return this card to your hand”  states: “Other Cards used in the combo (if any)  are sent to the discard pile.” If 2+ cards with  this effect are used in the same combo, do you  return both cards to your hand or only one? Answer:  Both. 3.   Discarded potions  Where do you place discarded potions? Answer:  To the discard pile near the potion  deck. 4.   Combo Can I perform special Effects from my Combo  in any order? Answer:  In any order - that doesn’t matter. 5.   Monster Card - Rotfiend  Does this supersede any other effects which  instruct me to draw additional or fewer cards  in phase III of my turn? Answer:  Only the Exploration card effect may  change the number of cards you draw during  phase III. If you Fight, you do not Explore.  Playing with Skellige expansion may be an  exception here - in that case, the player draws  1 card after defeating the Rotfiend, no matter  what the exploration card says. 6.   Grave hag’s first Fight turn  What is the order of suffering damage in the  Grave Hag’s first Fight turn? Answer:  Hand -> Shield -> Deck 7.   Raising attribute level as a reward  If you need to roll a die by any circumstances  and as a reward, you have to raise your attri - bute level as a reward, but you can’t (it’s al - ready at the maximum), do you get to reroll? Answer:  In that case, you don’t get any reward. 

4 8.   Level 5 attribute  Can the monster fight effect lower a level  5 attribute?  Answer:  No, it cannot. You can never lower  a level 5 attribute. 9.   Action card deck  Do you replenish the Action card deck? Answer:  During a 5-Player game, there is  a small chance that the Action deck will be  empty. In that case, Players should shuffle  the discard pile. 10.   Trail tokens Can a player have several trail tokens of the  same type? Answer:  No. One per each type. If you have  a mission for a trail and the exploration card  says: get a trail (same type), then you discard  your mission for a trail (you do not gain gold  from the trail token in that case!). 11.   Gain an action card  When an exploration card allows you to  gain an action card with exact value - does it  mean unmodified (printed) value or modified  (-1, +1)? Answer:  Unmodified. You use the modified  value only during Phase III of your turn. 12.   Next Exploration What does “During your next Exploration”  mean? Answer:  It means next time when you decide  to “Explore” in phase II. 13.   Ignore limit effect When I can draw any card (e.g., Potions)  ignoring the limit, how long does the “ignore  limit” effect last? Answer:  It works once, immediately. E.g., you  draw up to 5 potions. After you use one of  them, your limit is 4 again. 14.   Blizzard potion Blizzard potion ability: What is the meaning  of finishing a combo? Does it mean after  “playing a card combo” or “resolving a card  combo”? Answer:  After Playing a card combo.
