

Venturing through the islands to explore and study their mystical  creatures, you've stumbled upon gargantuan creatures lying dormant  throughout the island. These beasts, far exceeding the typical  creatures in size, seem to awaken when in the presence of others of  their kind, evoking an aura of balance to their surroundings.  Intrigued by these behemoths and their mysterious, potent abilities,  you embark on yet another thrilling expedition to unravel the  mysteries of these extraordinary creatures! introduction GAME DESIGNER Yeom C.W ARTIST Sophia Kang GAME DEVELOPERS Jaewoo Bang Gunho Kim ART DIRECTOR Hani Chang EDITOR Lucya Lee SPECIAL THANKS TO WONDROUS CREATURES  KICKSTARTER BACKERS https://badcomet.co info@badcomet.co
